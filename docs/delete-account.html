Phron · Privacy & support

Delete your account

You control your account.

Deleting your account permanently removes your Phron profile and associated account data. This action cannot be undone.

In the app

  1. Open Phron and sign in.
  2. Open Settings, then Profile, then Delete account.
  3. Follow the confirmation and identity-verification steps.

Without access to the app

Email us from the address linked to your account with the subject “Phron account deletion”. We may ask you to verify ownership before completing the request.

privacy@phron.com

What happens to your data

Your profile, friends, messages, game progress, and in-game balances associated with the account are deleted or anonymized. Limited security or legal records and residual backups may remain where needed. Local saves may remain until you clear app data.
