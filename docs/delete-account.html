Delete your Phron account

This page explains how to request deletion of your Phron account and the data associated with it.

Delete inside the app

  1. Open Phron and sign in.
  2. Open Settings → Profile → Delete account.
  3. Complete the confirmation and identity-verification steps.

Delete without access to the app

Email privacy@phron.com from the email address linked to the account. Use the subject “Phron account deletion”. We may ask for limited information to verify account ownership.

What is deleted

Your account, profile, friendships, messages, gameplay/social data, cloud progress, and uploaded avatar are deleted or anonymized where technically possible.

What may be retained

Limited purchase, fraud-prevention, security, legal-compliance, and residual backup records may be retained only for as long as necessary. Store transaction records controlled by Apple, Google, or RevenueCat are governed by those providers.

For questions, contact privacy@phron.com.
