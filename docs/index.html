Privacy policy

Updated 7 October 2026

Information you share

When you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your account identifier and the name, email, or profile photo you share. We also store your profile, messages, feedback, game progress, scores, and in-game balances.

How we use it

We use this information to run your account, connect you with friends, deliver games and messages, save progress, respond to requests, and prevent abuse. With your analytics choice, we collect app-use events to improve Phron. Crash reports and basic device and app information help us fix problems.

Who can see it

Other players can see your profile and game statistics and the messages you share with them. Providers that help with sign-in, data storage, analytics, and crash reporting process information needed for those services. Information may be processed outside your country. We may disclose information when legally required or to protect users.

Your choices

Edit your profile and privacy preferences in the app. Choose whether to share usage analytics. Camera and photo access are used for an avatar you choose; you can change these permissions in your device settings. Device authentication verifies account deletion; Phron does not receive your fingerprint or face data.

Ads and purchases

The current version does not show advertisements or offer real-money purchases. We do not sell your personal information or use it for personalized advertising.

Keeping and deleting data

We keep information while it is needed for your account and the services you use. You can request account deletion at any time. Associated account data is deleted or anonymized; limited security or legal records and residual backups may remain where needed. Local preferences and saves may remain on your device until you clear app data.

Privacy requests

You can ask about your information, request access, correction, or deletion, and exercise other rights available in your region. We use safeguards to protect information, though no system is completely secure. Phron is not designed for children under 13. Contact us if a child has shared personal information. Changes to this policy will appear here with an updated date.

Privatlivspolitik

Oplysninger, du deler

Sådan bruger vi dem

Vi bruger oplysninger til konti, venner, spil, beskeder, gemte fremskridt, hjælp og forebyggelse af misbrug. Afhængigt af dit analysevalg indsamler vi brugshændelser for at forbedre Phron. Fejlrapporter og grundlæggende enheds- og appoplysninger hjælper os med at løse problemer.

Andre spillere kan se profil, spilstatistik og beskeder, du sender dem. Udbydere af login, lagring, analyse og fejlrapportering behandler nødvendige oplysninger, eventuelt uden for dit land. Vi kan dele oplysninger, når loven kræver det, eller for at beskytte brugere.

Dine valg

Rediger profil og privatlivsvalg i appen. Vælg, om du vil dele brugsanalyse. Kamera og billeder bruges til en avatar, du vælger; styr tilladelser på enheden. Enhedsgodkendelse bekræfter kontosletning; Phron modtager ikke fingeraftryk eller ansigtsdata.

Reklamer og køb

Den aktuelle version viser ingen reklamer og tilbyder ingen køb for rigtige penge. Vi sælger ikke personoplysninger og bruger dem ikke til personlige reklamer.

Opbevaring og sletning

Vi gemmer oplysninger, så længe kontoen og tjenesterne har brug for dem. Du kan altid anmode om sletning. Tilknyttede data slettes eller anonymiseres; begrænsede sikkerheds- eller juridiske oplysninger og sikkerhedskopier kan blive bevaret ved behov. Lokale data kan blive på enheden, indtil appdata ryddes.

Privatlivsanmodninger

Du kan anmode om adgang, rettelse eller sletning og bruge rettighederne i din region. Vi beskytter oplysninger, men intet system er helt sikkert. Phron er ikke beregnet til børn under 13 år; kontakt os, hvis et barn har delt personoplysninger. Ændringer vises her med en ny dato.
